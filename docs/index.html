What it does

Built for vegetables, herbs, houseplants and hydroponic grows alike.

🌱

Every plant in one place

Search, filter and sort your plants by category, growth stage, medium, location and status.

💧

A detailed care journal

Log watering, feeding, measurements, climate, pests, training, transplants, harvests, stage changes and notes, with pH, EC and nutrient doses.

⏰

Know what's due

Set watering and feeding intervals per plant, mark tasks done in one tap, and get an optional daily reminder.

📷

Growth photos

Take or pick photos, browse the gallery and compare before and after. Stored copies have location data removed.

📈

Growth charts

See height over time, total growth, the average rate and how fast the plant grew over the last 7 days.

🧮

Grower tools

VPD, daily light integral, PPM ↔ EC conversion, a nutrient mixer, a unit converter and your own nutrient library.

Your data stays on your phone

PlantHelper has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and the Android app does not request internet access. Export a backup (.zip) or your journal (.csv) whenever you like, and delete everything from Settings.
